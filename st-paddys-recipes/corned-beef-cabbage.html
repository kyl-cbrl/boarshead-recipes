Boar’s Head First Cut Corned Beef Brisket St. Paddy’s Favorite

Classic Corned Beef & Cabbage

A traditional, cozy one-pot dinner: tender corned beef simmered with potatoes, carrots, and cabbage.

Serves: 6 Cook Time: ~3 hrs Style: One-Pot

Ingredients

  • 3–4 lb Boar’s Head First Cut Corned Beef Brisket (with spice packet)
  • 10 cups water (or enough to cover)
  • 1 large yellow onion, quartered
  • 4 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 4 large carrots, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1½ lbs baby potatoes
  • 1 small green cabbage, cut into wedges
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
Pro tip: Slice the brisket against the grain for the most tender bite.

Directions

  1. Rinse the brisket under cold water to remove excess brine.
  2. Place brisket in a large Dutch oven. Add water, spice packet, onion, garlic, bay leaves, and peppercorns.
  3. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer.
  4. Cover and cook for 2.5–3 hours until fork tender.
  5. Add potatoes and carrots. Simmer 20 minutes.
  6. Add cabbage wedges and cook 15 more minutes.
  7. Remove brisket and rest 10 minutes.
  8. Slice against the grain and serve with vegetables.
