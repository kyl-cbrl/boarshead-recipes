🍀 Classic Reuben Casserole

Serves: 6

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Boar’s Head Cooked Corned Beef, chopped
  • 2 cups Boar’s Head Sauerkraut, drained
  • 2 cups shredded Boar’s Head Switzerland Swiss Cheese
  • 1 cup Thousand Island dressing
  • 6 slices rye bread, cubed
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. In a greased 9x13 baking dish, layer chopped Boar’s Head Cooked Corned Beef evenly on the bottom.
  3. Spread drained Boar’s Head Sauerkraut over the corned beef.
  4. Drizzle Thousand Island dressing evenly over the sauerkraut.
  5. Sprinkle shredded Boar’s Head Switzerland Swiss Cheese generously over the top.
  6. Toss rye bread cubes with melted butter and scatter over the casserole.
  7. Optional: Sprinkle caraway seeds on top for extra rye flavor.
  8. Bake for 25–30 minutes until bubbly and golden.
  9. Let rest 5–10 minutes before serving.
