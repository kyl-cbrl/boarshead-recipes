Reuben Casserole

Serves 6–8
Oven: 375°F
Bubbly • Cheesy • Rye Crunch

Ingredients

  • 3 cups chopped cooked corned beef
  • 1 (14 oz) can sauerkraut, drained
  • 1 cup Thousand Island dressing
  • 2 cups shredded Swiss cheese
  • 6 slices rye bread, cubed
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds (optional)
Tip: Drain sauerkraut well so the casserole stays rich, not watery.

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a 9x13 baking dish.
  2. Layer half the rye bread cubes in the dish.
  3. Add corned beef evenly over the bread.
  4. Spread sauerkraut over the beef.
  5. Drizzle Thousand Island dressing across the top.
  6. Sprinkle Swiss cheese evenly.
  7. Top with remaining bread cubes.
  8. Drizzle melted butter and sprinkle caraway (optional).
  9. Bake 25–30 minutes until bubbly and golden.
  10. Rest 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
Serve with: a crisp pickle spear and extra dressing on the side.
