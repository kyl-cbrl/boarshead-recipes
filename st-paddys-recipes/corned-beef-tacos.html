🍀 Corned Beef Street-Style Tacos

Serves: 4 (8 tacos)

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Boar’s Head Corned Beef, chopped
  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage
  • 1/2 cup diced red onion
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Lime wedges for serving

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add chopped Boar’s Head Corned Beef and season with smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Sauté for 4–5 minutes until heated through and slightly crisp on edges.
  4. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave.
  5. Fill each tortilla with seasoned Boar’s Head Corned Beef.
  6. Top with cabbage, red onion, cilantro, and avocado slices.
  7. Squeeze fresh lime juice over tacos and serve immediately.
