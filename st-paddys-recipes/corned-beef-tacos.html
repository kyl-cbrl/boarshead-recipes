Corned Beef Street-Style Tacos

Crisp-seared corned beef tucked into warm tortillas with cabbage, onion, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime.

Serves: 4
Makes: 12 tacos
Time: ~20 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs cooked corned beef (thinly sliced or chopped)
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small white onion, diced
  • ½ cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • Lime wedges
  • Optional: crema or spicy mustard aioli
Upgrade idea: Add pickled red onions for a bright, tangy crunch.

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add chopped corned beef and sear until slightly crisp, 5–7 minutes.
  3. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet (or briefly over a flame).
  4. Fill tortillas with beef, cabbage, onion, and cilantro.
  5. Top with avocado and drizzle sauce (optional).
  6. Finish with fresh lime juice and serve immediately.
Serving tip: Keep tortillas warm in a towel so they stay soft and pliable.
